# DR-0003 · Units: positions are grouped into units in this stage

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV), in conversation with `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`
- **Sources:** the directions of the governing seat of 15 October 2026, translated: "The grouping of the work into units is to be done in PPD." And: "We have started a human process; bring it to its end."
- **Written by:** `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`

## Context

`v0.2.0` grouped steps and decisions into positions, and positions into reporting lines and forums, but named no units: no team, section or department with a head and a mandate. An organisation that launches without AI needs its units, and the grouping depends on the processes and positions this stage designs.

## Decision

1. **Units are designed here,** at S6 (Structure), before the reporting lines: positions are grouped into units under each part of the release, in the organisation's own words (team, section, department), each grouping with its reason.
2. **Each unit** has one head position from among its own positions, one parent unit, and a charter generated by code from what its positions hold: mandate, head, member positions, forums, measures and result line. A charter line that traces to nothing fails.
3. **Reporting lines follow the units** by default; a line that departs from them is a decision of the Requesting Authority.
4. **Release part A.15** (unit register and unit charters); TEST-PPD v1, cases 95 to 97.
5. **Positions only.** A unit names positions, never persons.

## Not decided here

The units of any organisation: each is a decision row of its Requesting Authority (blueprint §13, H-11).
