## What was studied

1. **A launch-readiness review** of what an organisation still needs, after this stage, to start working without AI. Three reviewer seats, each with its own web research. Most of what it found belongs to a separate stage after this one; three small additions belong here.
2. **A study of fifteen rival products** that design, launch or run organisations (transformation platforms, organisation-design and process tools, small-business operating systems, implementation and adoption tools), from public material only. Each useful feature was placed in the stage where it truly belongs. Rule applied to every feature: positions only; the organisation decides; a reference is a method, never evidence.

## Decision

The blueprint, the release parts and the fixed test carry these additions:

1. **The facts of each step** (§5.4, rule 11): when it is done; its worst error and whether it can be undone; the kinds of act it is; where its input comes from and how far it is trusted; how confidential it is.
2. **Risk, control and record per step, the controls catalogue and segregation of duties** (§5.4, rule 12; §5.5, rule 9; Annex A.11).
3. **A competence profile in each job description**, traced to steps and decisions (§5.5, rule 10).
4. **The objectives cascade**, from the decided goals to each position's measures; **measures of quality, time and cost** per process; **the result line** of each part (§5.4, rule 13; §5.5, rule 11; §5.6, rule 7; Annex A.12).
5. **Forum tiers and the review calendar** (§5.6, rule 6).
6. **Structure scenarios side by side**, with cost only from cost rates the organisation gives; the organisation chooses (§5.6, rule 8; Annex A.13).
7. **The peak-load check and end-to-end times** of processes and value streams (§5.4, rule 16; §5.7, rules 5 and 6; Annex A.14).
8. **Process variants** per entity, site or product (§5.4, rule 14).
9. **Configurable modelling conventions** (§5.4, rule 15).
10. **Reference models as methods only** (§5.3, rule 7).
11. **Approval and re-approval date per process** (§5.10, rule 7); the stage that runs the organisation raises the review.
12. Fixed test cases 78 to 94; configuration slots `re_approval_period`, `structure_scenarios` and `modelling_conventions`.

## Not taken

Anything that rates, ranks or watches a person; anything that acts on its own; decisions about who fills a position; outside benchmarks as evidence. Features whose home is another stage went there: the Target Operating Model (strategy one-pager, service delivery model, capability maturity, outside-party journeys, value case, priorities, activity analysis), Source Lifecycle (organisation dictionary, essentials checklist), the stage after this one (implementation, running and improving), and the AI stages (use-case candidates per step).

## Not decided here

The values of the new configuration slots (§13, H-09); the Library card change (`upstream/PROPOSALS.md`, item 5).
