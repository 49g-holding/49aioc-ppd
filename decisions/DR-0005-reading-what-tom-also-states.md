# DR-0005 · PPD v0.5.0: reading what the TOM release also states

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV), in conversation with `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` (the decider is a position)
- **Source:** the directions of GOV of 15 October 2026 (translated): "What can be given to Process and Position Design, give to it; what can be given to TOM, give to TOM; what remains, we must build", and "Where a capability's real home is in our earlier AIOCs, add it there too"; the direction of 16 October 2026 to go on in the agreed order (translated); TOM blueprint `v0.6.0` (its `decisions/DR-0006`; Annex H), which now states what this stage needs to start.
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0001 to DR-0004; it supersedes nothing.

## Decision

1. **Read, never filled.** The release-reading record covers the strategy frame, the outside parties' journeys, the value case and the activity profile, with each capability's delivery model and maturity, each stream's criticality and each element's decided priority. What the release states as `not given`, `not decided`, `not set`, `unknown` or `no observation` stays so (blueprint §5.1, rule 2).
2. **The objectives cascade starts from the strategy frame:** its one-year goals and critical numbers, down to each position's measures; a goal `not given` makes no row (§5.5, rule 11; Annex A.12).
3. **Critical streams first, in order of priority.** Processes on a critical value stream join the critical-process proposal; the order follows the decided priority of the elements each process carries; each process and position carries that priority into the release (§5.3, rule 5; §5.10, rule 9).
4. **As the capability is delivered.** In-house: its processes. Shared service: in the providing child, with a hand-off. Outsourced or partner: a hand-off and the oversight steps only, never an in-house process for the delivered work. Not decided: designed only as far as it is not needed, the rest returned (§5.3, rule 8).
5. **Journeys and maturity checked.** Each journey's moments sit in processes in the journey's order; a capability with a target maturity of 4 or 5 has a measure on each of its processes (§5.3, rules 9 and 10).
6. **Today's workload.** The activity profile is shown beside the designed workload, per process, and may be cited for a volume or a time; it is never about a person and changes no position (§5.7, rule 7; Annex A.9).
7. **What changes.** Blueprint header and one-page, §0, §1, §5.0, §5.1, §5.3, §5.5, §5.7, §5.10, §9, §12 to §14, §17, Annexes A, D and E (TOM `v0.6.0` pinned); TEST-PPD v3 (cases 105 to 111); the cards.
