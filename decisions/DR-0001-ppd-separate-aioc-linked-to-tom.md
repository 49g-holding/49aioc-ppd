# DR-0001 · Process and Position Design: a separate AIOC, linked directly to the Target Operating Model

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV), in conversation with `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`
- **Sources:** the directions of the governing seat of 15 October 2026, translated: "This is a separate AIOC. Its input is the target operating model itself, not Source Lifecycle; it is linked to the target operating model itself, because it may have defects and go back and forth. I do not want to merge it with the target operating model or with the stage after it." Then, on the proposal of the AI hub: "Good, yes. Only its full version, that of 49GAION: write its full name in the Library of 49GAION now, write that too, add its description to my Standard, and let us continue." Also the earlier directions of the same day: in these design stages no choice is made between what fills a position; and every human is a position, never a natural person (`49GAION/DR-0013`)
- **Written by:** `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`

## Decision

1. **A separate AIOC.** Process and Position Design, `[ORG]AIOC-PPD`, is an AIOC of its own. It is never merged with `[ORG]AIOC-TOM` or with the stage after it.
2. **The mother text.** This repository holds the mother blueprint of the Library part 49GAION Process and Position Design (Protocol §17; Standard §3.1, item 3; `49GAION/DR-0014`). It is public and holds no organisation data (Naming Standard R9).
3. **Linked directly to the TOM.** It takes the decided TOM release by reference and hash from `[ORG]AIOC-TOM`, and sends every defect it finds back there as a return. It never changes the release.
4. **Other sources** come only from Source Lifecycle, and every question to the organisation goes through its Chief of Staff.
5. **One stage, processes first.** Processes, decision rights and positions are designed together, processes first; job descriptions are generated from what each position holds.
6. **Positions only.** No natural person; no choice of what fills a position.
7. **Decided and filed.** Every element is decided by the organisation's Requesting Authority; the release is filed into Source Lifecycle and handed to the next stage by reference.
8. **Job families and levels.** The direction of the governing seat of 15 October 2026, translated: "What is the problem with having it? If no data comes for it, it is simply passed over." The position register carries each position's job family and level, from a source or a decision of the Requesting Authority; with neither, each reads `not given`. Nothing is invented. Pay stays outside this AIOC (blueprint §13, H-07).
